## Finished long-form

# The garment acquires an identity

Before imagining a universal product passport, read the textile standards and trademark records that already govern product identity.

## Then

China’s national standards portal records textile safety and apparel-use-information standards; these are product-governance records, not proof of a fashion-week passport system.

Evidence: SH-IDENTITY-THEN

## Now

CNIPA exposes a public trademark-search interface, but a query result must be saved and read before making any claim about a specific designer or mark.

Evidence: SH-IDENTITY-NOW

## Next—and the limit

No reviewed official source establishes that every Shanghai runway garment carries a shared digital identity, traceability record or future product passport.

Evidence: SH-IDENTITY-NEXT

Shanghai Fashion Week is not one technology. It is a chain of permissions that turns recognition into circulation and circulation into memory.
