## Governing question

## Canonical essay

Milan joins inherited house authority to contemporary systems of staging, craft, platform distribution, wholesale and traceability. Day 7 isolates one part of that system so spectacle cannot substitute for mechanism.

The historical record establishes a starting point, but it does not authorize a smooth story of inevitable progress. Italy’s public archive and museum surfaces, Modateca Deanna and Politecnico di Milano preserve different slices of fashion knowledge: records, objects, textiles and research.

That distinction matters because an institution can preserve an interface while losing the labor, contract, technical specification or permission trail that made it work. The public record is evidence of what was published, not a complete view of everything that happened.

The present layer is narrower and more useful when it keeps attribution attached. CNMI’s live gallery and video surfaces preserve selected official images and moving-image records, but discovery does not equal permission to reproduce.

Read operationally, this is a chain of actors, records and decisions. An organizer publishes or governs a surface; a venue, supplier, platform, brand, worker, press body, buyer or archive carries another part; and the audience usually encounters only the final visible state.

Technology here therefore means more than devices. It includes calendars, credentials, metadata, contracts, standards, interfaces, archives and the procedures that decide what becomes legible.
