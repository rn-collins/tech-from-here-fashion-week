The runway becomes a player

When the room becomes a player, which view becomes canonical?

Tokyo makes translation infrastructural across permission, venue, moving image, language, textile exchange, recognition and retained seasons. This chapter follows capture: The collection walks once; cameras, edits, metadata and rights agreements decide which view can be repeated and who can repeat it.
