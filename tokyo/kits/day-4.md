## Governing question

What must cross languages before a collection can cross markets?

## Canonical essay

Tokyo makes translation infrastructural across permission, venue, moving image, language, textile exchange, recognition and retained seasons. This chapter follows circulation: A public stream can widen technical access while ranking systems, language, bandwidth and platform ownership continue to govern attention.

The organizer operates Japanese and English interfaces across programme, brands, topics and organizational information. That earlier record fixes a point in time; it does not prove an inevitable progression toward the present.

A stream can be open while discovery remains privately ranked. Reach, access and attention need different evidence because they describe different systems.

The interface carries names, categories, access conditions and venue information across languages, but translation depth varies by record. The claim remains attached to its named source because public visibility is evidence of publication—not independent proof of every underlying practice.

Operationally, the chain runs through the organizer, broadcaster, platform, press desk, creator and viewer. Each controls a different record or decision, while the finished show encourages us to experience those decisions as a single seamless event.

Tokyo’s governing verb is translation. It is an editorial lens, not a claim about the city’s essence: it helps test how calendars, credentials, media, labor records, commercial data and archives become legible here.

No reviewed public record documents one season-wide translation workflow, glossary, error process or accessibility standard. A future plate is therefore a documented trajectory or an explicit open question—not a forecast manufactured from a press release.

This public record cannot establish what may remain private, paywalled, undigitized, untranslated or held at show level. That limit belongs in the published account because an evidentiary blank is not evidence of absence.
