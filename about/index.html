Method

Beauty has to show its receipts

Signal / Seam is an evidence preview, not a completed historical exhibition.

Four public states

Verified fact reports what a source directly establishes. Attributed claim preserves who said it. Editorial inference labels interpretation. Proposal names a reporting or design hypothesis. Unresolved stays visible when the record does not clear the threshold.

Research boundary

Public-surface research does not establish absence. Paywalled, undigitized, private, unstable, and untranslated records may exist. Search trails record where this edition stopped.

No synthetic evidence

No AI-generated image, synthetic quotation, invented archival material, unsupported superlative, or speculative future belongs in this system.
