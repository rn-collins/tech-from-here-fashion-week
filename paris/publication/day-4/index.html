PARIS · DAY 04 · LEGITIMACY

Paris rebuilt the week online

What changed when the official calendar acquired a digital twin?

Paris exposes the technical production of legitimacy through calendar inclusion, monumental rooms, official images, digital programmes, showrooms and archives. This chapter follows circulation: A public stream can widen technical access while ranking systems, language, bandwidth and platform ownership continue to govern attention.

FHCM states that Paris Fashion Week Online launched in 2020 with Launchmetrics and support from LE DEFI; official calendars now have physical and digital dimensions. That earlier record fixes a point in time; it does not prove an inevitable progression toward the present.

A stream can be open while discovery remains privately ranked. Reach, access and attention need different evidence because they describe different systems.

The digital platform expanded official collection visibility, but a public stream does not establish open admission, neutral ranking or permanent preservation. The claim remains attached to its named source because public visibility is evidence of publication—not independent proof of every underlying practice.

Operationally, the chain runs through the organizer, broadcaster, platform, press desk, creator and viewer. Each controls a different record or decision, while the finished show encourages us to experience those decisions as a single seamless event.

Designation comes before spectacle: the calendar, venue and official record decide what can appear as Paris fashion authority.

No permanent future supplier stack is inferred from the 2020 partnership or today’s public pages. A future plate is therefore a documented trajectory or an explicit open question—not a forecast manufactured from a press release.

This public record cannot establish what may remain private, paywalled, undigitized, untranslated or held at show level. That limit belongs in the published account because an evidentiary blank is not evidence of absence.

Paris Fashion Week, photographed by Kooyuh, is installed as a documentary witness under CC BY-SA 4.0. It supplies visual context only within the limits stated in its caption; it does not prove the three claims on this page.

Separate the room, signal, feed, recommendation layer and archive instead of treating “online” as one place. Then name what the record still cannot establish and keep the rights status beside the evidence.

This edition makes circulation legible as a relationship among people, interfaces and records—not a ranking of fashion capitals or a substitute for field reporting.
