What the week leaves behind

What survives, who can reuse it, and what never entered the record?

London makes the week visible as a relay among institutional schedule, temporary room, camera, platform, labor, commerce and archive. This chapter follows memory: The venue disappears, schedules change and links decay; preservation choices determine which technical history remains citable and contestable.
