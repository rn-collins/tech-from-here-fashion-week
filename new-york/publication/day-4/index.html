NEW YORK · DAY 04 · CLOCK

The sidewalk becomes a screen

When a private show reaches a public screen, which barrier has actually moved?

New York makes time itself operational: calendar authority, dispersed venues, capture, broadcast, production labor, orders and memory must coordinate. This chapter follows circulation: A public stream can widen technical access while ranking systems, language, bandwidth and platform ownership continue to govern attention.

CFDA launched RUNWAY360 with support for livestreams, AR/VR, 360-degree media and social integrations during the September 2020 digital pivot. That earlier record fixes a point in time; it does not prove an inevitable progression toward the present.

A stream can be open while discovery remains privately ranked. Reach, access and attention need different evidence because they describe different systems.

In September 2025, CFDA and Rockefeller Center announced public NYFW streams at the Rink and Channel Gardens, supported by Genesys. The claim remains attached to its named source because public visibility is evidence of publication—not independent proof of every underlying practice.

Operationally, the chain runs through the organizer, broadcaster, platform, press desk, creator and viewer. Each controls a different record or decision, while the finished show encourages us to experience those decisions as a single seamless event.

The city is the venue network; punctual coordination is what temporarily makes that network feel like one event.

No equivalent public-screen partnership for September 2026 is assumed until an organizer or venue publishes one. A future plate is therefore a documented trajectory or an explicit open question—not a forecast manufactured from a press release.

This public record cannot establish what may remain private, paywalled, undigitized, untranslated or held at show level. That limit belongs in the published account because an evidentiary blank is not evidence of absence.

NY Fashion Week, photographed by Anisa C, is installed as a documentary witness under CC BY 2.0. It supplies visual context only within the limits stated in its caption; it does not prove the three claims on this page.

Separate the room, signal, feed, recommendation layer and archive instead of treating “online” as one place. Then name what the record still cannot establish and keep the rights status beside the evidence.

This edition makes circulation legible as a relationship among people, interfaces and records—not a ranking of fashion capitals or a substitute for field reporting.
