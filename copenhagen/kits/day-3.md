## Governing question

What changes when teardown is designed before the first look appears?

## Canonical essay

Copenhagen turns participation into an evidence problem: admission, time, teardown, rights, screening, product data and institutional memory each demand a record. This chapter follows capture: The collection walks once; cameras, edits, metadata and rights agreements decide which view can be repeated and who can repeat it.

Current CPHFW terms require show-production measures including waste sorting, avoidance of single-use backstage plastic and zero-waste set design under the framework’s stated definition. That earlier record fixes a point in time; it does not prove an inevitable progression toward the present.

Most audiences meet a collection as an authored frame. Camera position, edit, caption, compression and licensing are part of the garment’s public meaning.

The terms permit a CPHFW representative to review backstage compliance on show day and allow additional documentation within a month; this is an operational evidence path, not an independent environmental audit. The claim remains attached to its named source because public visibility is evidence of publication—not independent proof of every underlying practice.

Operationally, the chain runs through the designer, photographer, videographer, editor, image agency and archive. Each controls a different record or decision, while the finished show encourages us to experience those decisions as a single seamless event.

Copenhagen’s governing verb is verification. It is an editorial lens, not a claim about the city’s essence: it helps test how calendars, credentials, media, labor records, commercial data and archives become legible here.

Show-level material inventories, energy use, transport boundaries, carbon calculations, suppliers and teardown outcomes remain unavailable unless brands or producers release them. A future plate is therefore a documented trajectory or an explicit open question—not a forecast manufactured from a press release.

This public record cannot establish what may remain private, paywalled, undigitized, untranslated or held at show level. That limit belongs in the published account because an evidentiary blank is not evidence of absence.

For every remembered look, identify the frame-maker, owner, distribution channel and surviving file. Then name what the record still cannot establish and keep the rights status beside the evidence.
