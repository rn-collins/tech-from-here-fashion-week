# TFH-CPH-D3 — The set must account for its exit

**Status:** evidence-backed production draft; recording, interviews, specialist review and owner-final approval remain open

## Governing question

What changes when teardown is designed before the first look appears?

## Finished long-form

# The set must account for its exit

In Copenhagen, the set is asked to account for what happens after it disappears.

## Then

Current CPHFW terms require show-production measures including waste sorting, avoidance of single-use backstage plastic and zero-waste set design under the framework’s stated definition.

Evidence: CPH-SET-THEN

## Now

The terms permit a CPHFW representative to review backstage compliance on show day and allow additional documentation within a month; this is an operational evidence path, not an independent environmental audit.

Evidence: CPH-SET-NOW

## Next—and the limit

Show-level material inventories, energy use, transport boundaries, carbon calculations, suppliers and teardown outcomes remain unavailable unless brands or producers release them.

Evidence: CPH-SET-NEXT

Copenhagen’s distinction is not a claim of purity. It is the decision to make participation depend on evidence—and the obligation to keep asking what that evidence cannot establish.
