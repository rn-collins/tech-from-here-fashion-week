## Governing question

## Finished long-form

# Proof becomes a threshold

A Copenhagen runway begins before the room: with a file that must clear an admission threshold.

## Then

CPHFW introduced its framework in 2020 and has required applicants to document compliance with Minimum Standards for official schedule consideration since January 2023.

Evidence: CPH-ENTRY-THEN

## Now

For SS27, the organizer says 34 show and presentation brands were reviewed by its show committee and a Rambøll-led sustainability committee against 19 Minimum Standards.

Evidence: CPH-ENTRY-NOW

## Next—and the limit

CPHFW states that its process validates submitted information but is not a certification scheme or audit; admission must not be restated as proof that a brand or product is sustainable.

Evidence: CPH-ENTRY-NEXT

Copenhagen’s distinction is not a claim of purity. It is the decision to make participation depend on evidence—and the obligation to keep asking what that evidence cannot establish.
