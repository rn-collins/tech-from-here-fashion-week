## Governing question

Who establishes the canonical view, and under what rights?

## Canonical essay

London makes the week visible as a relay among institutional schedule, temporary room, camera, platform, labor, commerce and archive. This chapter follows capture: The collection walks once; cameras, edits, metadata and rights agreements decide which view can be repeated and who can repeat it.

Runway photography and broadcast do more than document a collection: their framing helps establish the version that circulates and survives. That earlier record fixes a point in time; it does not prove an inevitable progression toward the present.

Most audiences meet a collection as an authored frame. Camera position, edit, caption, compression and licensing are part of the garment’s public meaning.

The official London Fashion Week platform combines event pages with designer-controlled films and BFC video channels, making moving image part of the event’s attributable public record rather than an after-the-fact add-on. The claim remains attached to its named source because public visibility is evidence of publication—not independent proof of every underlying practice.

Operationally, the chain runs through the designer, photographer, videographer, editor, image agency and archive. Each controls a different record or decision, while the finished show encourages us to experience those decisions as a single seamless event.

London’s governing verb is relay. It is an editorial lens, not a claim about the city’s essence: it helps test how calendars, credentials, media, labor records, commercial data and archives become legible here.

The bounded search in G-FUTURE-07 did not establish a documented BFC deployment of generative image production; search and classification are not mislabeled as generation. A future plate is therefore a documented trajectory or an explicit open question—not a forecast manufactured from a press release.

This public record cannot establish what may remain private, paywalled, undigitized, untranslated or held at show level. That limit belongs in the published account because an evidentiary blank is not evidence of absence.

London Fashion Week SS2012 at Somerset House, photographed by Knox387, is installed as a documentary witness under CC BY-SA 3.0. It supplies visual context only within the limits stated in its caption; it does not prove the three claims on this page.

For every remembered look, identify the frame-maker, owner, distribution channel and surviving file. Then name what the record still cannot establish and keep the rights status beside the evidence.

This edition makes capture legible as a relationship among people, interfaces and records—not a ranking of fashion capitals or a substitute for field reporting.
